# Cover artwork

These 79 JPEGs are the **original box art, key art and arcade flyers** for the games in the directory. They are used only so a card is recognisable at a glance; they are not generated placeholder images.

* Where the repository's earlier catalogue already contained the correct artwork for a port, that image was carried over.
* The remaining covers were sourced from public cover-art archives: MobyGames, Spong, Wikimedia/Wikipedia, publisher key art and archived retail listings. Sources include official front covers for Super Mario 64, Super Mario Odyssey, Mario Kart Wii, Crash Team Racing, Forza Horizon 2, The Wind Waker, Jak & Daxter, Metroid Dread, Donkey Kong Country 1–3, EarthBound, Star Fox, F-Zero, Time Crisis, Virtua Fighter 2, Gears of War, God of War, Gran Turismo Sport, The Order: 1886, Zelda II, GTA: Liberty City Stories, Mario Party 4, Syphon Filter, Wave Race 64, Ninja Gaiden II, Tekken 3, Xenoblade Chronicles X and the Sega/Namco arcade flyers.
* Images are stored as 960×540 JPEGs. Portrait box art is composited over a blurred, darkened copy of itself so the full cover stays visible in a 16:9 card.

All artwork, characters, logos and trademarks belong to their respective publishers, developers and rights holders. This is an unofficial, non-commercial directory; nothing here implies endorsement, and no game files, ROMs or ISOs are included in this repository.
