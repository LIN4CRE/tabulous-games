Under the hood

One bot, four jobs, every 24 hours

The catalogue maintains itself. No team, no editors, no manual link-checking — just a scheduled workflow with guard rails.

View the workflow
01

Scan

PortedGames’ daily changelog, its full browser-port index, the Spawn feed filtered to survivors-likes, and a curated watchlist.

02

Verify

Every candidate link is fetched and every existing card is re-checked for 404s, timeouts and domains hijacked to unrelated sites.

03

Publish & repair

Up to three fully verified finds are added per run. Dead entries are retired into the audit log — with a network-outage guard so a bad day never wipes the catalogue.

04

Report

A heartbeat file powers the status panel on this page, everything is committed automatically, and GitHub Pages redeploys itself.

The maker

Built and maintained by one developer.

There is no studio behind Tabulous Games — no team, no funding, no ad network. Every card description, control list and caveat was written by hand, and the automation that keeps the links honest was written in the evenings by a solo developer.

The bot handles the tedious part: it checks the links, spots the dead hosts and logs every removal. What it cannot do is judge whether a game is worth your evening — that still takes a person, which is why the catalogue stays hand-curated while it grows automatically.

Browser controls & pro tips

Most of these games are real C/C++ engines compiled to WebAssembly, drawn with WebGL or WebGPU. A few browser conventions apply to all of them.

FeatureHow to use it
Pointer lockClick inside the game to capture the mouse for 360° aim. Press ESC to release it.
AudioBrowsers mute autoplay — click or press a key once and the audio context starts.
FullscreenUse the game’s own fullscreen toggle, or F11 / Ctrl+Cmd+F.
ControllersXbox and PlayStation pads work over USB or Bluetooth through the Gamepad API.
SavesProgress lives in browser storage for that origin. Clearing site data deletes it — export saves where the game offers it.
PerformanceEnable hardware acceleration and close heavy tabs; WebGPU titles need a current Chrome, Edge or Safari.
